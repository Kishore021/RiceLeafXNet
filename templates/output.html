Tips for Better Image Analysis

Good Lighting

Capture the rice leaf in sufficient lighting so its visible features can be clearly observed.

Clear Image

Avoid blurry photographs. Keep the camera steady while capturing the rice leaf.

Leaf Visibility

Keep the affected rice leaf clearly visible within the photograph.

Correct Image

Upload a rice leaf image rather than unrelated plants, objects or heavily obstructed photographs.

How RiceLeafXNet Analyzes the Image

The uploaded image is prepared for the selected deep learning model and passed through the trained classification network. The model generates class probabilities, and RiceLeafXNet displays the predicted rice leaf class to the user. Logged-in users can also review their previous scan results through the application.
